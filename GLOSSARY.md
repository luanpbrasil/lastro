# Lastro

Lastro provides context for economic and political claims in videos and written posts. Post-related language reflects the agreed scope of the X integration being designed.

## Claims and context

**Claim**:
A statement attributed to a source's author, including a video's speaker or a post's writer, that a viewer may want to understand in context.
_Avoid_: Verified truth, model opinion.

**Context card**:
A presentation of an attributed claim grounded in a source passage, with available intellectual context, opposing views, readings, or official figures.
_Avoid_: Truth score, verdict on a speaker.

**Fact**:
A claim about the past or present that can be checked. A fact card may remain unchecked when no supported official figure applies.
_Avoid_: Proven claim.

**Theory**:
A causal or normative claim about how the economy, society, or a policy works.

**Forecast**:
A prediction or policy proposal about a future outcome.

**Quote**:
The source passage used to ground a card in the author's words.

**Paraphrase**:
A neutral restatement of the claim that attributes it to its author.

**Headline**:
A short title naming the claim on a card.

**Knowledge graph**:
A curated map of schools of thought, thinkers, institutions, and historical episodes, with relationships and readings.
_Avoid_: Complete map of political opinion.

**Stance**:
The claim author's relationship to a view: relying on it, echoing it, or contradicting it.
_Avoid_: The viewer's position, a judgement of correctness.

**Opposing view**:
A view that disagrees with the attributed claim, taking the author's stance into account.
_Avoid_: Opponent of any named school regardless of the speaker's position.

**Episode**:
A historical event that provides context for an idea's application or consequences.

**Official indicator**:
A supported economic measure published by an official source and used to contextualize a numerical claim.

**Numerical comparison**:
A comparison of a claimed value with the official value for the relevant measure and period, reported as matching, differing, or unchecked.
_Avoid_: A judgement that an entire argument is true or false.

## Transcripts and viewing

**Captions**:
Timed text supplied with a YouTube video, either automatic or authored.

**Live transcript**:
Text recognized from the audio as a viewer listens to a video.

**Transcript segment**:
A stretch of transcript text associated with a start time and duration in the video.

**Coverage**:
The portions of a video already claimed for analysis, including work still in progress.
_Avoid_: Proof of transcript accuracy, count of completed cards.

**Listening session**:
A temporary opportunity to contribute transcript text for one video.
_Avoid_: User account, personal viewing history.

## Written posts

**Post**:
A publication on X selected by a viewer as the source of claims to contextualize.
_Avoid_: Tweet.

**Post text**:
The selected post's own written content, distinct from embedded quoted posts and attached media.

**Relevant post**:
A post containing an economic or political claim that Lastro can help a viewer understand in context.

**Empty analysis**:
A completed analysis that found no relevant claims in the selected post text.
_Avoid_: Failed analysis.
